**U12-01 — Classification and scope.** Approval B shall use an expressly authorized, bounded, cumulative event-support accounting regime distinct from V2-2B experimental accounting and actual lifecycle accounting. This regime governs support produced or admitted for its authorized acquisition, processing, transfer, reconciliation, accounting, reporting and bounded finalization. Governed protected material remains subject to ABE’s inventory, route, custody, protection and retention requirements regardless of support-accounting classification.

**U12-02 — Separate obligations.** Classification determines applicable rules; allocation assigns capacity; reservation commits sufficient capacity in advance; charging records accountable production or admission; reconciliation establishes supported correspondence with actual events; finalization establishes the bounded endpoint and required evidence status. Satisfaction of one obligation shall not be represented as satisfaction of another.

**U12-03 — Accountable population.** Every relevant production and admission route shall be declared with supported bounds or excluded on evidence. Coverage shall include acquisition, transfers, transformations, copies, governed containers, framing and metadata, platform/framework outputs, temporary and diagnostic artifacts, accounting, reporting, failure and terminal production. Artifacts used as evidence or support shall not escape accounting through their labels.

Protected source objects, acquired instances, support artifacts and ambient runtime state shall be distinguished by justified scope determinations. Reading an existing source does not by itself classify that source as a newly created support artifact. Conversely, an instance outside support accounting remains governed wherever ABE’s protected-material rules reach it. Neither ambient objects nor runtime state are automatically included or exempted merely by name.

**U12-04 — Class A, B and C.** ABE’s GPM classes remain controlling:

- Class A instances have the required known and authorized route, custodian, destination class, bounded population, protection and retention treatment.
- Class B instances retain their full bounded potential reservation/charge while creation is uncertain. Completeness remains unproven until evidentiary reconciliation.
- A route requiring Class C unknown or unbounded production, custody, destination or retention is not authorizable.

Class C arising during the event, or unreconcilable Class B, receives the adopted whole-event stop treatment. Conservative charging cannot repair completeness or authorize continuation.

**U12-05 — Express allocation and supported fit.** Before Approval B authorization, the exact proposed event shall have an express finite allocation, supported reservations and independently reviewed worst-case fit. Applicable count, per-object, aggregate and binding role limits shall be satisfied independently.

The showing shall cover every permitted path through required original evidence production and finalization, including uncertainty, failures, reporting, transfer and receiver production within the event’s boundary. Mutually exclusive branches may share a maximum only where exclusivity is established. No unknown population or size may be treated as zero or bounded by the allocation’s mere declaration.

Any earlier concrete allocation commitment requires prior applicable closure. The allocation shall not be inherited from the narrow profile, an experiment, actual lifecycle capacity or unused capacity elsewhere. This resolution assigns no numerical value.

**U12-06 — Reservation deadline and failures.** Full worst-case cumulative capacity for the event, including required failure and final evidence, shall be reserved before its first governed effect. Any earlier applicable deadline remains controlling. Dynamic instances may arise only within declared classes, operations, destinations and reserved maxima.

The design shall provide bounded behavior when an execution-observable fact defeats an expected successful path. An actual source size may be learned during the authorized event only where prior design sufficiency already establishes bounded acquisition and failure behavior. A failed bound check shall not permit truncating required evidence, treating a partial acquisition as complete, retrying, or silently enlarging the allocation. Credible support for every mandatory output remains required before authorization.

**U12-07 — Charging and uncertainty.** Accountable creation or admission incurs its applicable charge. Separate creation, recreation, replacement, copying, exporting into a separate artifact, derivation and creation of a governed container shall be charged separately. Packaging shall not merge previously distinct charges.

Repeated references may count once only on admissible sameness or continuity evidence. Unresolved identity receives no deduplication benefit. Conservative separate accounting shall remain distinguishable from proven distinctness.

Each object shall retain its maximum incurred charge over the accounting interval. Deletion, shrinking, replacement, phase change, branch selection or termination shall not refund incurred capacity. Uncertain creation retains its full potential reservation/charge unless admissible evidence resolves the uncertainty; such resolution shall not erase a charge actually incurred.

**U12-08 — Capacity breach.** Exceeding an applicable reservation or limit is a capacity failure. It creates no authority to enlarge the allocation, borrow capacity, subdivide the activity, truncate evidence, split a report to evade limits, create a substitute representation or retry. Required stopping shall not depend on successful reporting. Incomplete closure may be recorded only through already authorized bounded means.

**U12-09 — Reconciliation and independent support.** The concrete design shall identify the producer evaluated for every production, transfer, receiver, accounting and finalization claim and the independent evidentiary basis for that claim. Effective capability unions shall be assessed before independence is asserted.

Reconciliation shall connect the declared routes and populations to original admissible evidence of actual creation, admission, transfer, receipt, transformation and charges. It shall preserve partial, adverse, conflicting and uncertain outcomes. It shall not rest solely on the evaluated producer’s account, a caller-supplied inventory, reciprocal attestations or agreement within one effective capability union.

Accounting reconciliation shall not substitute for AR-1 source binding, AR-2 completeness or AR-3 acquisition coherence. Each required relationship retains its own proof obligation.

**U12-10 — Finite finalization.** Required accounting, reconciliation and finalization production, including its independent evidentiary support, shall be covered by a finite, predeclared design and advance reservation. The applicable R-22 order shall be preserved:

1. Ordinary dispatch ends.
2. Terminal actions are attempted once or finally marked withheld or unavailable.
3. Each slot’s reporting disposition freezes, separately from execution state.
4. Each source record’s status is established.
5. The source-write route is fenced before final source-state capture.
6. Source artifacts are sealed.

After sealing, no slot dispatches and no terminal-action record first appears. Final governed objects shall be established and charged before the declared creation frontier; no new governed artifact of that closed domain may arise beyond it. These requirements prescribe no particular objects, sizes, slots, records or implementation.

**U12-11 — Final evidence and missing records.** No final record shall certify its own production, write, synchronization or delivery. Independent support shall terminate in admissible grounding bases rather than an endless succession of self-certifying records.

A successor record shall not repair a missing required final record. Missing required final evidence leaves the relevant closure incomplete or unproven. Where reporting is possible, unresolved operations, last known holders, open effect intervals, blocked terminal actions and known or possibly arriving capabilities shall remain ascertainable. Complete reporting shall not be represented as successful execution or physical quiescence.

**U12-12 — Accounting boundary and retention.** The event accounting interval shall include all support required for its original acquisition, admissibility, transfer, normalization, reconciliation, accounting, reporting and closure, regardless of producer or label. Declaring a creation frontier shall not remove an unfinished original obligation from that interval.

Retention and protection duties may continue after production closes, under the established design and until separate release authority. Closure, failure and stopping grant no cleanup, deletion, overwriting, movement, release, disposal or further inspection authority.

**U12-13 — Crossings and receiving domains.** Every proposed export, transfer, receipt, transformation and admission shall identify its accounting boundaries and applicable obligations on each side. A boundary crossing shall transfer neither live identity nor unused capacity. Receipt, validation, acceptance for a stated use, admission and retention remain distinct.

Receiving domains apply their own authority, provenance, currentness, inventory, capacity and charging rules. Substantive reliance requires the applicable admission and charge; calling it a citation grants no exemption. Copies, derivatives and containers receive their own applicable charges.

The narrow receiving-domain allowance remains global: at most eight admitted-input objects and 524,288 bytes across the narrow identity domain, with independent per-object and lower applicable limits. These are not Approval B allocations. Oversized required input remains a capacity failure; a favorable excerpt cannot substitute for required evidence.

**U12-14 — Mandatory outputs and later work.** The ABE mandatory-output set shall remain intact. M-1 through M-6 are read-stage outputs; M-7 remains mandatory separate tailoring work based on admitted inputs; M-8’s prohibition on source or package acceptance remains controlling.

M-7’s absence at read completion is not itself a read failure, but the complete staged package shall not be reported complete while M-7 is pending. Its production, derivation, admission and accounting require their own applicable authority and treatment. Staging creates no free capacity and no authority to revive the original event.

Genuine later review occurs after the relevant domain closes, under separate authority and accounting. It shall remain distinguishable from original evidence. Evidence required for the original domain shall not be relabeled as later review, and later review shall not supply missing original observations or repair missing final evidence.

**U12-15 — Premises and grounding.** Every material premise shall carry its evidence class, timing kind, one proposition-specific dependency tag, supplying authority/event, required deadline and discharge status.

Design-validity premises must be established before closure. Pre-effect premises may be scheduled only under all CR-29 conditions. Execution-observable premises may be carried only under CR-30, with no advance assertion that they hold. Earlier deadlines prevail over the general first-dependent-effect floor.

Every closure claim shall have a complete non-circular support line under CR-37–42. Adopted text and specification may establish rules and design commitments; they shall not be used as proof of empirical conformance. Reviewer acceptance, Human Chair adoption and unsupported supplied values are not substantive evidence.

**U12-16 — Proposition-specific dependencies.** No universal ordering among UQ-4, UQ-9 and UQ-12 is established. A route commitment remains subject to UQ-4; adoption of a dependent PB/custody design remains subject to UQ-9. A B5-dependent proposition cannot close while G1 is HOLD. An actual Q-parent proposition requires its own P0-dependent support; successful P0 qualification separately remains a predecessor to Approval B authorization.

U-4 and U-6 block the exact propositions within their unresolved scope. P0 output shall not enter the Approval B envelope, and Approval B output shall not feed back into P0. A closure claim shall not depend on a later-operational fact.

**U12-17 — Closure and decisions.** Every concrete accounting/allocation element shall satisfy CLOS GC-1–GC-12 and applicable class-specific requirements before first commitment. Mutually reliant items may be considered together only with claim-level grounding.

Concrete closure requires the complete showing, eligible independent verification without unresolved blocking findings, and the Human Chair’s recorded determination for the exact item or set. Closure does not itself select, adopt or authorize.

Before incorporation into Approval B, the exact package shall receive the required independently verified coherence evaluation, including complete mandatory-output support and explicit conditional-premise status. Package evaluation is not a separate Human Chair determination and is not Approval B. Prior concrete adoption or first commitment through incorporation remains subject to the existing CLOS paths.

It would establish no concrete population, numerical allocation, fit result, route, verifier, ledger, enforcement arrangement or finalization mechanism. Those obligations remain separately open until satisfied through their governing processes.

Any later Human Chair determination resolving UQ-12 at this governance level shall expressly preserve those concrete obligations and the distinction between design sufficiency and execution reconciliation. Original defects include hidden dependencies, circular support, unsupported bounds, omitted requirements and defective independent verification.

Suspension propagates to dependent claims and affected package evaluations. Renewed closure requires the applicable revised showing, whole-revision verification and fresh determination. Original records and adverse findings remain preserved. Reopening authorizes no acquisition, inspection, cleanup or operational action.
